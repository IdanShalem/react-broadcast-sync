## Why react-broadcast-sync?

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Hook or provider" icon="rocket">
    Use `useBroadcastChannel` for a channel local to one component, or `BroadcastProvider` to share
    one channel with your whole tree through context.
  </Card>
  <Card title="See who is online" icon="magnifier">
    Ping the channel to discover active tabs, and give each tab its own identity with `sourceName`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Control the traffic" icon="setting">
    Filter by message type, isolate channels with namespaces, expire stale messages, and keep only
    the latest message when that is all you need.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Type-safe and tested" icon="approve-check">
    Written in TypeScript with full type inference, covered by unit and cross-browser cross-tab
    tests.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
